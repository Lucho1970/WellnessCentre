# Welcome, new clients

We want your first booking to be straightforward. You can browse services and available times before creating an account.

## How online booking works

1. Choose the visit type, service, practitioner, and appointment length.
2. Select a date and an available time.
3. Sign in or create your secure client account.
4. Review your information, price, and appointment details before confirming.

Selecting a time while browsing does not reserve it. Availability is checked again when you confirm.

## If the clinic already has a record for you

Ask the clinic to send you an invitation instead of creating another client record. For your protection, an email address match alone does not link an online identity to an existing record.

## Before your appointment

Your practitioner or the clinic will provide any preparation instructions and required forms that apply to the selected service. Contact the clinic if you are unsure which service to choose.
