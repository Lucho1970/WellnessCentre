# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need an account to view available appointments?

No. You can browse services and available times without signing in. You need a secure client account before confirming an appointment.

## Is a time reserved when I select it?

No. The system checks availability again when you confirm the appointment.

## What is an On-Site appointment?

An On-Site appointment takes place at the client’s location. It is available only for practitioners and services configured for mobile visits. The visit address must be within the practitioner’s service area, and a travel surcharge may apply.

## What if the clinic already has a client record for me?

Contact the clinic and request an account invitation. Do not create a duplicate record solely because you use a different email address.

## Where can I manage my appointments?

After signing in, open the client portal to view your appointments and account information.

## How do I get help?

Use the [contact page](/contact) to find the clinic’s published phone number or email address.
