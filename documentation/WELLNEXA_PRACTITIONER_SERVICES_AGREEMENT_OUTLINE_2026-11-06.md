# Wellnexa practitioner services agreement — counsel drafting outline

**Not an agreement; do not publish or collect acceptance.** Prepared 6 November 2026 for 9553282 Canada Ltd operating as Wellnexa. The first planned discipline is registered massage therapy. Applications from other professions may be received, but activation requires a profession-specific legal, clinical, insurance, record and retention review. The practitioner and clinic will enter a written agreement before clinical access.

Counsel and the owner should draft exact English and French text covering:

1. Parties, legal names, independent contractor versus employment status, effective date, term, renewal, termination and notice.
2. Scope of services and allowed professions; proof and ongoing status of Ontario registration, insurance, scope of practice and sanctions; virtual-care location and professional obligations.
3. Fees, collections, taxes, payment timing, refunds, chargebacks, trial or subscription terms, and who issues patient receipts.
4. Wellnexa group-practice structure and actual health information custodian/agent roles; responsibility for clinic records, separate-practice records, access/correction, breach cooperation, patient restrictions and clinic closure/succession.
5. RMT-specific record handling and storage, access on departure, and what happens if employment/contract ends, the practice closes, moves or is sold, as required by the current [CMTO record-keeping standard](https://www.cmto.com/rules/standard-of-practice-record-keeping/). The standard states retention of patient health records for ten years after the last visit, or ten years after the patient turned 18 if under 18 at the visit. Confirm the current standard and exact facts before configuring a schedule.
6. Scheduling, cancellations, continuity and handoff, patient notices and lawful transfers; no automatic bulk export of clients or notes to a separate practice.
7. Privacy/security duties, training, approved tools, audit, incident response, access hold and formal departure; subcontractors and personal devices.
8. Intellectual property, branding, advertising, complaints, dispute resolution, governing law and required notices.
9. Signature method, evidence retention, delivery of an executed copy, amendments and renewal. Review the [Ontario Electronic Commerce Act](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/00e17) and profession-specific requirements. Electronic form requires the parties' consent; an electronic signature may meet a signature requirement where the Act applies. The current in-app acknowledgement ledger stores version/hash, verified identity, language and time, but counsel must decide whether that is sufficient for this services contract or whether a dedicated e-signature workflow is needed.

**DocuSign option:** evaluate only after legal, privacy and security review of provider terms, document/identity fields sent, geography/support access, retention/deletion, incident terms, authentication, callback verification and executed-copy storage. This branch adds no DocuSign dependency, endpoint or external data transfer. A simpler in-app signature may be acceptable if counsel approves the method and the executed copy/evidence design. The existing `services_agreement` document kind is storage plumbing, not an approved signature workflow.
