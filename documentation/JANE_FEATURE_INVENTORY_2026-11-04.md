# Jane feature inventory — 4 November 2026

**Purpose:** Publicly documented Jane capabilities for product comparison. This is a research snapshot, not a specification, purchasing recommendation, or verification of a Jane account. Jane changes its product; features vary by country, subscription, add-on, provider eligibility and rollout. “Jane offers” below means Jane's official site or Guide describes the capability, not that every clinic can enable it. The [comparison](JANE_FEATURE_COMPARISON_2026-11-04.md) checks this repository separately.

**Source method:** Jane's [feature index](https://jane.app/features) and [Guide index](https://jane.app/guide), accessed 4 November 2026. The links at the end of each section lead to Jane's own feature or Guide pages. The inventory covers the major product domains and notable subfeatures on those pages; it cannot enumerate every account setting, country-specific payer, integration or newly released beta. Jane uses “patient”; Copihue commonly uses “client.”

## 1. Discovery, booking and scheduling

- **Online booking:** Public treatment, practitioner and location selection; real-time available slots; clinic controls for who/what/when can be booked; branded booking links or an embedded booking path; client self-booking and staff booking.
- **Booking rules:** Staggered/rolling availability, service duration and buffers, new-versus-returning patient restrictions, booking lead times, cancellation/reschedule windows and policy acknowledgements. Configurable card collection, deposits or prepayment work with Jane Payments.
- **Appointment lifecycle:** Confirm, reschedule, cancel, mark arrived/no-show/completed; calendar status/colour cues, appointment notes/history, balances and schedule search; drag/drop schedule changes and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Staff calendar:** Recurring shifts, breaks, time off and coverage of multiple providers/locations; room/resource assignment and conflict avoidance; privacy mode; external calendar synchronization.
- **Group services:** Classes/group appointments with capacity and attendance workflows, subject to the configured service and plan.
- **Wait list:** Staff and patient wait-list entries, preferred practitioner/service/time, openings and notifications/offers, with staff management of matches.
- **Patient app:** Jane for Clients mobile app supports booking and managing visits across participating Jane clinics, wait-list activity, messages, forms and virtual appointments. Availability and supported actions depend on clinic setup.

Sources: [online booking](https://jane.app/features/online-booking), [scheduling](https://jane.app/features/scheduling), [wait-list Guide](https://jane.app/guide/category/wait-list-management), [patient app](https://jane.app/features/patient-mobile-app), [online-booking Guide](https://jane.app/guide/category/online-booking).

## 2. Client and clinic operations

- **Profiles:** Patient demographics, contact details, appointment and billing history, insurance policies, documents, chart and communication history; staff/practitioner profiles with role-based access.
- **Client self-service:** Patient portal for appointments, forms, invoices/receipts, payment requests and secure messages; rescheduling/cancellation where permitted.
- **Intake/forms:** Custom intake, history, consent, surveys and insurance-information forms; automatic assignment around booking, manual sends and reminders; completed answers flow into the patient record; clinic/practitioner templates and permission controls.
- **Notifications:** Email and SMS confirmations, reminders and schedule-change notices; forms and payment reminders; message settings/templates and delivery history. Exact triggers/channels vary by setup.
- **Access/security operations:** Staff access levels, practitioner-specific settings and privacy controls; Jane's security page describes its own safeguards, which are not a substitute for evaluating this application's controls.
- **Migration:** Jane provides import guidance for moving records from other practice systems; import scope and manual work depend on source data.

Sources: [intake forms](https://jane.app/features/intake-forms), [notification Guide](https://jane.app/guide/category/notifications-reminders), [patient-profile Guide](https://jane.app/guide/category/patient-profiles), [staff access](https://jane.app/guide/staff-access-levels), [import Guide](https://jane.app/guide/category/importing-to-jane).

## 3. Care records and communication

- **Clinical documentation:** Custom chart templates, SOAP/narrative notes, reusable phrases/fields, treatment plans, chart review and signing/supervision, linked forms/surveys and chart audit history.
- **AI Scribe:** Optional recording/dictation-assisted draft notes for clinician review, editing and signing. This is a separate privacy, consent and clinical risk decision, not a baseline scheduling feature.
- **Secure messaging:** Authenticated patient–practitioner conversations linked to the patient record, read receipts, attachments/file sharing, notifications and access through portal/app; workflows for archive/history.
- **Telehealth:** Video visits integrated with the schedule and patient workflow, patient joining links/virtual waiting, screen sharing/chat and group/family sessions. Group capacity is an optional add-on and actual availability can differ by plan.
- **Patient feedback:** Surveys, ratings/reviews and publication/marketing controls; professional advertising rules need local assessment.

Sources: [documentation](https://jane.app/features/clinical-documentation), [AI Scribe](https://jane.app/features/charting-ai-scribe), [secure messaging](https://jane.app/features/secure-messaging), [telehealth](https://jane.app/features/telehealth), [telehealth Guide](https://jane.app/guide/category/telehealth), [ratings and reviews](https://jane.app/features/ratings-and-reviews).

## 4. Sales, billing and insurance

- **Invoices and receipts:** Appointment-linked invoices, separate product sales, detailed/basic receipts delivered by email/print/patient portal, adjustments, refunds and outstanding-balance tracking.
- **Payments:** Jane Payments integrates online and terminal transactions, card on file, secure payment requests, cancellation fees, payouts and reconciliation. Card processing and rates vary by country; this inventory makes no price claim.
- **Products and inventory:** Product catalogue, stock/reorder controls, taxes, sales and commissions.
- **Packages and memberships:** Prepaid visit bundles, recurring memberships, eligibility/usage/expiry rules, online or clinic purchase, redemption at booking and remaining-balance reporting.
- **Gift cards:** Sale and redemption through Jane's booking/payment workflow, subject to configured rules.
- **Canadian insurance:** Insurer and patient policy setup, split patient/insurer responsibility, billing codes, claims/invoices, insurer statements and payments. Jane's Guide documents TELUS eClaims eligibility/submission/reversal, Pacific Blue Cross PROVIDERnet, BC Teleplan and manual insurer portal/paper workflows. **TELUS eClaims requires Jane's Insurance add-on on eligible plans**; payer and professional eligibility must be confirmed. BC-specific integrations are not Ontario baseline requirements.
- **US insurance:** Eligibility, electronic claims and remittance via US-specific integrations are separate from the Canadian path and are not an Ontario launch parity target.
- **Payroll:** Jane advertises employee/contractor pay runs, direct deposits, CRA remittances, paystubs and year-end forms. Jane states Canadian availability on specified plans excluding Quebec and describes a beta waitlist; verify actual availability before treating this as a present clinic option.

Sources: [invoicing and sales](https://jane.app/features/invoicing-and-sales), [Jane Payments](https://jane.app/features/jane-payments), [packages and memberships](https://jane.app/features/packages-and-memberships), [gift-card Guide](https://jane.app/guide/category/gift-cards), [Canadian insurance Guide](https://jane.app/guide/category/insured-visits), [Jane Payroll](https://jane.app/features/jane-payroll), [US billing](https://jane.app/features/billing-and-insurance).

## 5. Reporting, marketing and ecosystem

- **Operational and financial reports:** Appointment volume and outcomes, practitioner/location performance, invoices, receivables, transactions/payouts, reconciliation and export/filter controls.
- **Practice growth reports:** Return visits, unscheduled patients, referrals, retention and service patterns; Jane's available definitions and plan limits need account-level verification.
- **Website and discovery:** Optional Jane Websites builder, booking links, search optimization tools and review/ratings features; these are marketing products, not required to run a Jane schedule.
- **Integrations:** Jane publishes an integration catalogue for connected tools; each provider, data flow, geography and commercial term needs separate evaluation.
- **Human reception service:** Jane advertises trained virtual assistants/receptionists. This is a service offering, not software feature parity.

Sources: [reporting and analytics](https://jane.app/features/reporting-and-analytics), [Jane Websites](https://jane.app/features/websites), [Jane SEO](https://jane.app/features/seo), [integrations](https://integrations.jane.app/), [virtual assistants and receptionists](https://jane.app/features/admin-and-receptionists), [feature index](https://jane.app/features).

## Interpretation for our comparison

“At least what Jane offers” needs a scoped baseline. Jane combines core scheduling with optional clinical, payments, marketing and country-specific products. A useful launch comparison should first test **book → remind → intake → visit → invoice/receipt → follow-up**, plus privacy/roles and daily staff operations. Other entries remain visible in the comparison so we can decide which are required at launch, which are later, and which do not fit Copihue's model. No Jane feature or implementation approach is incorporated by reference into our requirements.
