# Wellnexa record governance — implementation decision and release gates

**Status:** partial implementation on `feature/wellnexa-record-governance`; synthetic pilot only. Dated 6 November 2026. This is a project decision and implementation specification, not a legal determination of custody or permission to use real patient information. The accountable custodian and an Ontario health privacy/clinical reviewer must approve the actual practice arrangement, profession-specific duties and final wording. Production is unchanged.

## Agreed operating model

For care delivered as Wellnexa care, the intended custodian is **9553282 Canada Ltd operating as Wellnexa**, subject to legal confirmation that it operates the relevant group practice. Practitioners act on its behalf for that care, use only information needed for assigned duties, and remain responsible for their own professional obligations. This is custody/control, not a claim that the clinic owns a person or may sell patient information. Notes created in the clinic portal are intended to form part of the clinic record even when authored by a practitioner. An external practitioner practice's earlier records remain separate unless specific information is lawfully supplied to Wellnexa. No client relationship or prior record moves solely because a practitioner joins or leaves.

This model does **not** apply automatically to independent tenant clinics. Each tenant needs its own custodian/agent/platform agreement and notice. No shared support role gains clinical access from this decision.

## Clinical notes and sharing

The baseline schema contained `practitioner_client_notes`, but source inspection found no baseline note read/create/delete API. Its name identifies the author, not legal custody. No delete workflow should be created. Corrections or addenda should preserve the original, author, time and reason. A practitioner may read their own clinic notes only with a current patient-care relationship and current account authority. Reading another practitioner's note may be permissible for authorized direct or follow-up care, subject to necessity, patient restrictions and professional duties; it is not a clinic-wide default or an entitlement based solely on past appointments. This branch adds explicit care-team assignment and read/insert rules for the new note API; clinical/privacy sign-off is still required before enabling it in production.

Current access rules still use a canceled or historic appointment and successful client creation as continuing relationship evidence in some places, and booking contact/address lookup can reach unrelated active clients. This must be narrowed at each server read, list, search, preview, export and worker boundary before real PHI. Existing patient restrictions continue to apply. Do not silently alter existing hosted patient access until migration and care reassignment are reviewed.

## Immediate access hold versus formal departure

Migration 062 and the accompanying Admin portal/API change implement a **reversible emergency hold** for a local practitioner account. A clinic-wide Super Admin or Clinic Admin can lock an active practitioner account using one of three non-PHI reason categories. A Super Admin alone may restore it if the account remains locked by that hold. Existing workforce and external-staff membership authentication require `users.status='active'` on protected requests, so a locked account is denied. A separate status edit cannot reactivate an active hold. The mutation, reason category and actor are audited atomically with the account change. It does not cancel bookings, alter clinical records, notify patients or complete a departure. The operator must review existing sessions, in-flight work and external provider access in hosted acceptance.

The **formal departure** workflow remains to implement: record effective date and responsible reviewer; reassign/cancel future care and follow-ups; notify affected patients as appropriate; handle requests for access/copies/transfer; revoke all identities, memberships and provider permissions; preserve histories, retention, legal holds and audit. A practitioner must not receive a bulk export simply because they brought the clients. A clinic closure/succession process is also required.

## Outside-practice intake and patient rights

Prior records may be imported only through a reviewed, minimal, patient-specific intake documenting source custodian, source record, recipient clinic, purpose, authority/consent evidence, items received, time, operator and integrity hash. The system must not copy an entire practice by default. Files require format/content verification, size limits, private storage, malware control and authorized retrieval. The source practitioner's retention obligation is independent of the copy held by Wellnexa. Cross-practice disclosures, patient access/correction requests, refusals, protected delivery and transfer requests require a tracked process with the applicable response deadlines. A record-class/profession-specific retention and hold schedule is required; no automatic deletion on departure or account closure.

## Practitioner documents and acceptance

The bilingual [records agreement](WELLNEXA_PRACTITIONER_RECORDS_AGREEMENT_DRAFT_2026-11-06.md) and [confidentiality/departure acknowledgements](WELLNEXA_PRACTITIONER_CONFIDENTIALITY_DEPARTURE_DRAFT_2026-11-06.md) are for owner, legal and clinical review. They must not be represented as final terms or silently substituted for a services/employment agreement or treatment consent. The new workflow records exact published EN/FR version/hash, displayed language, authenticated identity, clinic, timestamp and audit correlation. Prior versions and acceptances remain stored. The new notes API rejects unsigned and stale versions. Wider PHI gating and secure copy delivery are still needed. Review Ontario's Electronic Commerce Act and profession-specific signature requirements before release.

## Implemented in this branch and still incomplete

Owner clarification on 6 November: registered massage therapy is the first planned profession; other professions may apply, but each requires its own record, college and retention review before clinical activation. Wellnexa intends a written practitioner agreement. The [services-agreement drafting outline](WELLNEXA_PRACTITIONER_SERVICES_AGREEMENT_OUTLINE_2026-11-06.md) records the unresolved terms and signature decision. `services_agreement` is supported as a versioned document kind, but no actual agreement is published. DocuSign remains an unapproved optional provider; this branch adds no external data flow.

The new clinic-notes API requires a current clinic-scoped care-team assignment, a current active practitioner account, no patient restriction, and acceptance of every current published practitioner document. Notes are author-only by default; the author may expressly share a note with the current care team. Another assigned practitioner may then read it while their access remains current. Existing notes default to author-only. The API provides insert/read only, audits reads and creation, and uses a request key to avoid duplicate note creation after uncertain responses. A clinic or super administrator controls assignments. Client merges with any care assignment are blocked pending reviewed migration. Clinical/privacy approval remains required before deployment.

The bilingual [records agreement](WELLNEXA_PRACTITIONER_RECORDS_AGREEMENT_DRAFT_2026-11-06.md) and [confidentiality/departure acknowledgements](WELLNEXA_PRACTITIONER_CONFIDENTIALITY_DEPARTURE_DRAFT_2026-11-06.md) remain **drafts only**. The new Admin workflow can store and publish reviewed bilingual versions; the practitioner workflow captures exact version/hash, displayed language, clinic, authenticated identity subject hash, timestamp and audit correlation. No legal draft is seeded or published. The new notes API rejects unsigned or superseded versions. Other practitioner PHI paths are **not yet gated** by document acceptance, and secure copy delivery remains to implement.

The older client list, booking contact/address, form and scheduling routes still treat historical appointments, client creation or delegated scheduling as continuing access in some cases. The new note boundary does not repair them. Existing clinic relationships must be reviewed by the responsible clinician before care assignments are entered; no bulk assignment backfill is authorized. Outside-record intake, patient access/correction case tracking, class/profession-specific retention/hold, and formal departure remain unimplemented. These are release blockers for real patient information.
