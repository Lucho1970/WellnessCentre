## What matters first

Jane's software covers a complete daily practice loop. For the first Wellnexa clinic, the highest-priority gaps to decide are **waitlist**, **clinical charting or an approved external record handoff**, **actual telehealth delivery**, and **appointment-to-invoice/payment completion**. The first two may alter clinical/workflow safety; payment requires merchant and accounting decisions. Existing booking, forms and communications slices also require hosted validation before real users. These are recommendations for discussion, not changes to the committed roadmap.

## Decisions for the owner review

1. Is the first launch a booking/administration pilot with records and video handled by approved external services, or a single-system practitioner workspace? This sets the charting and telehealth minimum.
2. Must clients pay online at booking/after visits, or can staff record outside payments and issue receipts initially? Which entity is merchant/receipt issuer for each practitioner?
3. Is an automated waitlist needed at launch, or is a controlled manual staff list acceptable for the first clinic?
4. Are group visits, insurer direct billing, packages, products and native app access required for the first clinic, or later optional modules?
5. Which Jane capabilities must be included in every tenant tier versus priced as add-ons (SMS, advanced finance, video, insurance, etc.)?
