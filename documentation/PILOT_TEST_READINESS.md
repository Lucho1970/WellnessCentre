# Limited external testing readiness

This is a **development pilot**, not production launch approval. The deployed public site, portal, and API support testing with approved synthetic or otherwise authorized records. Do not ask testers to enter sensitive clinical histories, payment details, or information that depends on unfinished forms, notes, billing, or messaging workflows.

The current evidence and open gates are recorded in the [30 September practitioner beta readiness audit](PILOT_READINESS_AUDIT_2026-09-30.md).

## Before inviting testers

1. Record the deployed package manifest and verify that the private API, public site, and portal point to the same release. Confirm the site's HTTPS, branding, client sign-in, and staff sign-in in a fresh browser session.
2. Back up the development database. Confirm test practitioner assignments, working hours, service durations/prices, travel radius, booking horizon, and cancellation policy. Use test-only clients and appointments where possible.
3. Complete the hosted MySQL booking race acceptance in [Booking validation tests](BOOKING_VALIDATION_TESTS.md), using the [temporary hosted test suite](HOSTED_TEST_SUITE.md) when CLI access is unavailable. Record database/server versions and the result; a skipped race or local browser mock does not close this gate.
4. Confirm the mail scheduler is healthy, a test confirmation arrives, and the Email status page shows no unresolved `needs_review` events. `sent` means accepted by Graph, not necessarily delivered. Verify a change/cancellation email and its calendar attachment with a test mailbox. After reminder migration 021, check a future test booking's 24-hour reminder row and its reschedule/cancellation behavior before relying on reminder delivery.
   After notification-review migration 022, verify that only Clinic Admin/Super Admin can open a `needs_review` item, that both evidence checks are required, and that an old SMS or email offers no automatic retry. Do not generate a live provider failure solely to test this screen; use a naturally failed test notice or a separate disposable database.
   On the Operations dashboard, compare the all-time needs-review count with Notification status. Follow **Review items**, confirm it opens the filtered list, then try Email/SMS and Today/Last 7 days filters. A notification older than seven days must remain in the all-time count until reviewed. The last-seven-days accepted count should include provider acceptances from the previous calendar week.
   After scheduler-health migration 023 and its matching private API, public cron pointer, and portal are deployed, wait for one Azure recurrence. The Operations dashboard and Notification status should show a recent successful scheduled run and the clinic-scoped overdue count. A booking's immediate send alone must not update the scheduled-run time.
5. Confirm the clinic's contact email is configured. The client and staff portals show **Report a problem**, which opens an email draft to that address with the page path, UTC time, and prompts for steps, expected versus actual result, and any on-screen correlation/reference ID. Query strings and fragments are omitted to avoid copying sign-in or invitation secrets. If the clinic email is not configured, the link opens the public contact page instead. Do **not** ask testers to send passwords, tokens, clinical details, or screenshots showing another person's data.

## Suggested pilot journeys

| Tester | Journey | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New client | Sign in, register, review profile, find and book a time | Only their own data appears; price, visit type and appointment number are clear. |
| Returning client | Sign in, view, reschedule, cancel, download calendar file | Their own appointment changes are reflected in the portal and email. |
| Practitioner | Sign in, view day/week/month calendar, manage own availability and booking; review time-off impact | Only authorized schedule and client details appear; privacy mode hides names when screen sharing. Time-off review identifies overlapping bookings without canceling or moving them. |
| Practitioner travel estimate | With an On-Site booking in the next 24 hours, open the dashboard; check first from clinic, then grant or deny device location for a current-location check | No route request occurs on page load; current-traffic distance/time and a suggested departure appear only after a button click; denial does not prevent clinic-origin checking; unrelated practitioners cannot fetch the route. |
| Practitioner day-of workflow | After migration 024 and matching private API/portal deployment, open the dashboard with a confirmed test booking today; use the call link, record and undo visit steps, book a follow-up, then mark completed or no-show on separate test bookings | Only the assigned practitioner sees contact and milestone actions. Steps are optional, timestamped, and do not notify clients. Outcome changes appear in appointment history; no-show is blocked after a started session. A completed On-Site visit may still record Left residence. |
| Practitioner visit history | Open an appointment with recorded and corrected visit steps in **Appointments → More details**, including after the visit day; open an unrelated practitioner's appointment by URL | The assigned practitioner sees newest-first Record/Undo history, while another practitioner is denied. History is manual activity, not GPS or clinical proof. |
| Past visit outcome | On a test visit from yesterday, use **Appointments → Past → More details** to mark completed, undo it, then mark no-show; attempt no-show on a visit with an active Started step | Only the assigned practitioner can change outcomes. The list refreshes after each change; an active session blocks no-show, and undo is limited to two days after the scheduled end. |
| Seven-day visit activity | On the Practitioner dashboard, compare the visit counts with controlled appointments from the last seven local dates; record then undo a step, refresh, and follow the **Needs visit outcome** link | Counts are practitioner-scoped and reflect current appointment outcomes and active optional steps. An undone step is not counted; missing steps are not treated as missed care. The linked filter shows only ended confirmed/rescheduled visits and removes a visit after its outcome is saved. |
| Practitioner time-off follow-up | Save a future time-off block overlapping a controlled booking, open the Practitioner dashboard, follow its appointment link, then reschedule or cancel it | The warning names the number of future affected bookings, opens only the practitioner's appointment, and clears after the dashboard is refreshed. Saving time off alone does not notify the client. |
| Practitioner client follow-up | Open **More details** on an assigned test appointment, then attempt a direct link to another practitioner's booking | Contact email, phone and preferred method appear only for the assigned booking; the unrelated booking is denied. The appointment list itself does not include contact fields. |
| Practitioner notifications | Open My notifications, filter Email/SMS and status, select a notice and follow its appointment link | Only notices addressed to the signed-in practitioner appear; review/retry controls never appear. Another practitioner's notice and the clinic-wide admin route are denied. |
| Super Admin/reception | Manage service assignments and book for a test client | Duration/price choices, practitioner, base location and room constraints are enforced. |

For each journey, check a narrow/mobile viewport, refresh, sign-out/sign-in, expired session behavior, and one failed or canceled action. Record failures with correlation IDs before changing data; this makes API log review possible.

## Known scope limits

Forms/clinical notes, secure messaging, waitlists, invoicing/payments, external practitioner calendar synchronization, and full launch/security/accessibility sign-off are not complete. Calendar `.ics` attachments may require recipients to accept or import the event. Do not present these modules as available to pilot users. Keep the pilot small and supervised until booking concurrency, mail, and role boundaries are verified on the hosted system.
